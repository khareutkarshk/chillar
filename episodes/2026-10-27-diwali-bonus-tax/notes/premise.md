# Premise — Diwali bonus pe tax

## Spine (no jokes)
1. A cash Diwali bonus is taxed like salary (F1).
2. It sits on top of your yearly salary, so it is taxed at the slab it lands in — slabs run 0% to 30%, no one flat rate (F2).
3. Maan lo ₹15 lakh salary + ₹1 lakh bonus, new regime, only std deduction, cess ignored: extra tax ₹15,000, ~₹85,000 stays (F9).
4. Someone on ₹9 lakh + ₹50,000 bonus pays ₹0 (rebate up to ₹12.75 lakh for salaried) (F10, F3).
5. TDS is the employer's projection-based withholding; the bonus month can be more/less; the final tax is settled in the ITR (F6).

## 8 candidate games
1. **TDS is mummy taking the Diwali lifafa: "beta, main rakh leti hoon."** Heighten: bonus = just another lifafa (salary) → the more lifafe you got, the bigger cut from the last one (slab) → cousin with fewer lifafe keeps all (rebate) → twist: mummy never gave the hisaab, ITR does.
2. Bonus is a wedding guest who brings the taxman as plus-one. Heightens weakly; doesn't explain slabs.
3. Salary is a building, bonus is the top floor, taxed at that floor's rent. Clear, but no laugh engine, no voice.
4. TDS is the hostel warden confiscating snacks "till exams end". Explains withholding, not slabs.
5. Bonus is a Diwali sale price: MRP on mail, real price after "charges". Implies hidden fees — wrong mechanism.
6. Canteen folklore "30% katega" as a fake WhatsApp forward debunked thrice. Myth vs Math energy, but no character.
7. Employer as an over-careful dad who keeps cash "andaaza lagake". Okay, but mummy version is more recognised.
8. The bonus mail as a trailer, the credit as the movie. One joke, no three steps.

**Pick: #1.** Every Indian kid has lived it, it *is* withholding (kept, not eaten), it maps to slabs (bigger total → bigger cut from the last envelope) and to the rebate (the cousin who keeps everything), and it pays off with a callback: mummy never settled the hisaab, the ITR does.
