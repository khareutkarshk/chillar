## Hook
Bonus ka mail aate hi phone cart mein daal diya. Account mein aayega kitna? [JOKE] {chillar: side-eye}
Diwali lifafa pe mummy bolti thi, "Beta, main rakh leti hoon." [JOKE]
Bonus mein bhi wahi scene, bas mummy ka naam ab TDS hai. [JOKE] {meme: M2}

## Setup {field: note20}
Tax ke liye bonus salary jaisa hi hai [F1]. {stamp: "Bonus = salary"}
Baaki salary pe judta hai. Slab zero se tees percent tak hain [F2].
Jitne zyada lifafe milte, aakhri wale se utna bada hissa jaata. [JOKE]

## Escalation {field: note50}
Maan lo: new regime, ₹15 lakh salary, ₹1 lakh bonus [F9].
Extra tax: lagbhag ₹15,000 [F9]. {stamp: "₹15,000"} {visual: slip fine print "sirf standard deduction, cess ignored"}
Haath mein lagbhag ₹85,000 [F9]. Phone ab "save for later" mein hai. [JOKE] {meme: M1}
Maan lo dost: ₹9 lakh salary, ₹50,000 bonus. Rebate se tax zero [F10][F3].
Usko mummy ne poora lifafa de diya. Mujhe aaj tak nahi. [JOKE] {chillar: crying}

## Payoff {field: note500}
Credit kam aaya? Company ne bonus nahi kaata.
Employer saal ka andaaza lagake TDS kaatta hai [F6].
HR ka "TDS as applicable" matlab: "Beta, main rakh leti hoon." [JOKE]
Kitna bachega, tumhara slab batayega, aur hisaab ITR mein hoga [F2][F6].
Mummy ne hisaab kabhi nahi diya. ITR deta hai. [JOKE] {chillar: facepalm}
