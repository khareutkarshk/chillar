# Premise — gold kabhi girta nahi (round 1)

Spine: (1) Ghar ka myth: "sona kabhi girta nahi". (2) RBI ki rate list (jewellers' association ka 24-carat rate) mein sona 6 hafte mein ~5% gira [F6], 2016–17 mein 7 mahine mein ~5.6% [F7]. (3) So what: girta hai, aur phir bhi das saal pehle se kaafi upar ho sakta hai (~4.9× [F12]). (4) In ₹: 10 g = ₹30,070 = maan-lo fresher ke 1.5 mahine (2016) → ₹1,47,250 = 4.9 mahine, ~41% saal (2026) [F10, F11]. (5) Do: din ka rate dekho, bill mein sona / making / GST alag maango.
takeaway: Sona girta bhi hai, chadhta bhi; 10 gram ab 1.5 nahi, 4.9 mahine ki salary.
follow_up: "Toh abhi gira hai, abhi le lein?" → koi nahi jaanta; sabke paas sirf pichla chart hai.

## 8 candidate games
1. **Didi vs me, refereed by gold** — mummy compares my first salary with didi's 2016 one; the yardstick is 10 g in months of pay. Heighten: didi 1.5 mahine → me 4.9 → 41% of my year → myth flips (it fell) → I pay more months, so who's sanskaari now? ✔ PICKED
2. Chacha's WhatsApp forward fact-checked against RBI receipts — good for the myth, weak for the months comparison.
3. Gold priced in "months of my life" (a jeweller who bills in months) — clear, but no people, no act-out.
4. Salary takes the stairs, gold the lift (and lifts go down) — two images, the lift-down part is shaky.
5. Gold as a topper cousin who "never fails" but flunks unit tests — implies a long-run promise we can't source.
6. The jeweller's taraazu: 10 g vs my salary slips on the other pan — strong visual, kept as a visual note only.
7. Gold "breathing" (chacha's excuse for every fall) — becomes the twist's act-out inside game 1.
8. Dhanteras shopping list as a time machine to 2016 prices — gimmicky, teaches less.

Why 1: the comparison *is* the explanation (same 10 g, same family, two years, two salaries), three true steps that get worse (1.5 → 4.9 → 41% of a year), the myth sits naturally in mummy's/chacha's mouths, and the payoff flips mummy's comparison. Retold to a parent — "didi ko 10 gram 1.5 mahine ka pada, mujhe 4.9 ka, aur beech mein sona gira bhi" — still correct.
