# Premise — salary-12-days (round 1)

Spine: (myth) "Salary kisi ek bade kharche ne kha li." (mechanism) Maan lo ₹40,000 aaye; 12 din mein 7 alag kharche — rent, card bill, food, outings, sale, UPI, autopay — ₹34,247 le gaye. (so what) Koi ek villain nahi; sabse chupi leak autopay hai jo bina poochhe har mahine katti hai. (in ₹) ₹5,753 bache = ~₹320 roz; subscriptions ₹1,347/mahina = ₹16,164/saal. (do) RBI rule: autopay se ≥24 ghante pehle message aata hai, aur PIN/OTP check ke saath ek debit ya poora mandate band kar sakte ho — SMS padho.

Candidates:
1. Salary ki gumshuda report — I'm at the thana, expecting one big chor; the line-up has 7 small ones, and the quietest had the house key (autopay = permission I gave). Heightens: big obvious suspects → "bas ek tha" small ones → the one who never knocked. RBI = it must ring the bell 24 h early; you can take the key back.
2. Office samosa plate — everyone takes "bas ek", plate empty. Heightens via bigger "bas ek"s; weak on autopay.
3. Salary is a shaadi ka lifafa being passed around relatives — each takes shagun. Cast-heavy, autopay doesn't map.
4. Salary is a hostel Maggi packet shared with the floor — funny, but "who took it" ends without a mechanism.
5. Salary as a phone at 100% battery, apps draining in background — autopay = background app. True-ish, but battery isn't rupees and it's a tech metaphor, not a story.
6. Salary as a leaking tanki on the roof — 7 taps; plumber-style. Honest but no voices, no act-out.
7. Salary as a cricket team all-out for 34 — each batter "sirf 12 run diye". Mapping inverted (runs = spends), confusing.
8. Salary's own farewell party — every kharcha gives a speech. Funny, but no investigation = no reveal.

Pick: #1. The investigation *is* the explanation (myth = looking for one chor; truth = seven small ones), has a natural three-step heighten, and the autopay "chor jo darwaza nahi khatkhata, kyunki chaabi maine di" maps 1:1 to an e-mandate; RBI's 24 h notice = the doorbell, opt-out = taking the key back. True when retold.
