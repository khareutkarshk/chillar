# Premise — No Cost EMI ka hidden cost (30 s, round 1)

## Spine (no jokes)
1. "No Cost EMI" sounds like a free loan.
2. RBI said zero-percent interest doesn't exist; interest gets camouflaged as processing fee [F1].
3. Example: ₹60,000 phone, 6 months, assumed 15% → ₹2,652 interest [F3].
4. In "no cost" that ₹2,652 sits in the price, or in the cash discount you give up [F3].
5. Rule: put the EMI total next to the cash price — the gap is the interest.

## 8 candidate games
1. **Interest is a kid playing hide-and-seek behind the curtain — feet showing.** Heighten: hides in the fee (RBI literally says "camouflaged") → hides in the price/discount (₹2,652) → I couldn't find it and bragged "free mein pada" → payoff: pull the curtain.
2. The treat-wala friend: "aaj meri taraf se", split request at 1 AM. Heighten: fee = split, price = he added his cab. Funny, but "treat" doesn't explain *where* the cost goes as clearly.
3. A magician: interest vanishes from one hand, appears in the other. Clear, but a magician is a generic image; no observed truth.
4. Hostel mess "free extra roti" that's already in the monthly fee. Only one hiding place; weak escalation.
5. Restaurant "complimentary dessert" + service charge. Same as 4.
6. "Free parking" with minimum purchase. Conditional, not hidden — wrong concept.
7. Relative's "kuch nahi chahiye beta" — generic prop (comedy.md §4). Killed.
8. Interest as an ex who "changed": same person, new outfit (fee/price). Close to 1 but romance angle is tired.

**Pick: 1.** The concept *is* "the interest didn't go anywhere, it hid" — and RBI's own word is "camouflaged", so the game is literally the source. Three hiding spots = three heightening steps; curtain callback pays it off.
