## Hook
Poore mohalle ki kaju katli ka bill kitna?
Papa ke hisaab se, ek dabba. [JOKE] {chillar: side-eye}
"Arre, ek hi dabba toh hai."

## Setup
Maan lo ek dabba ₹600 ka hai [F4].
Phir group mein list banti hai. {visual: phone note list growing name by name}
"Mamaji, padosi, aur woh aunty jinka naam kisi ko nahi pata." [JOKE] {meme: M2}
Maan lo list mein tees ghar [F5].
Ek hi dabba, tees baar: ₹18,000 [F6]. {stamp: "₹18,000"}
List abhi live hai. Mamaji ne saale bhi jod diye. [JOKE]

## Escalation
Is baar list chhoti karni thi.
Papa ne note khola: "teesri manzil wale. Pichhli baar bada dabba diya tha."
Matlab yeh gift nahi, udhaar hai. Bada karke chukana padta hai. [JOKE] {chillar: facepalm}
Main list kaatne baitha tha. Bua ka naam add karke utha. [JOKE]
Badaam-pista pe GST barah se paanch percent ghata [F1], pichhle saal hi [F2].
Tax sarkar ne kaata. List kisi ne nahi. [JOKE]

## Payoff
Ulta karo, pehle total, phir divide.
Maan lo budget ₹12,000, tees ghar mein baanto: ek ghar ka ₹400 [F7]. {stamp: "₹400 / ghar"}
List wahi rahegi. Bas dabbe ka aura kam hoga. [JOKE]
Ab bolo, "ek hi dabba toh hai." Bas ₹400 wala [F7]. [JOKE] {chillar: smug}
