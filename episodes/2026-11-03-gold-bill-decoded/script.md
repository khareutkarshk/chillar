## Hook
Sona aaj ke rate pe, bill itna kyun?
Mummy ne rate ka viva bhi liya tha. [JOKE] {chillar: side-eye}
Paper syllabus ke bahar se aaya.

## Setup
Maan lo, das gram sona, value ₹1,20,000 [F8]. {visual: bill slip prints the gold line}
Pehla sawaal making charge, matlab banai ki fees.
Uncle bole, "Making bas thodi si hai, 12%." [F8]
Thodi si matlab ₹14,400 [F8]. [JOKE] {stamp: "₹14,400"}
Maine "thoda kam?" bola, unhone ladoo pakda diya. [JOKE] {meme: M1}

## Escalation
Phir GST, matlab sarkari tax, 3% poore bill pe, making samet [F1][F2].
"Thodi si" making pe bhi ₹432 tax [F8].
Sarkar sochti hai, bill itna ho hi gaya, 3% aur sahi [F1]. [JOKE] {meme: M3}
Sone ke upar ₹18,432 extra [F9]. {stamp: "3% sab pe"}
Out-of-syllabus sawaal, aur uspe negative marking bhi. [JOKE] {chillar: facepalm}

## Payoff
Aur compulsory sawaal, kai shehron mein HUID, woh toh maine padha hi nahi. [JOKE] {chillar: crying}
HUID matlab har gehne ka six-character ID, roll number jaisa [F4].
Gehne pe laser se likha hota hai [F4], bill se milao.
Papa bole, "Chalis saal purana jeweller hai."
Bharosa chalis saal ka, check chhe character ka. [JOKE]
Yeh BIS ke sarkari Verify HUID se check hota hai [F5].
Bill upar isliye, kyunki making bhi judti hai, aur sab pe 3% [F8][F2].
Mummy ne poocha, "Rate yaad tha?" Haan, bas syllabus alag tha. [JOKE]
