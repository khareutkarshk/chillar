# Premise — gold-bill-decoded (round 1)

## Spine (no jokes)
1. Myth: making charge pe alag 5% GST lagta hai, aur HUID bas ek sticker hai. [F2, F4]
2. Mechanism: bill = sone ki value + making charge (banai ki fees), aur 3% GST is poore total pe. [F1, F2]
3. So what: making ke har rupaye pe bhi 3% tax lagta hai; making jeweller ka number hai — wahin paisa chhupa hai. [F8]
4. In ₹ (maan lo): ₹1,20,000 sona + ₹14,400 making + ₹4,032 GST = ₹1,38,432; ₹18,432 sone ke upar. [F8, F9]
5. Do: itemised bill lo; HUID gehne pe aur bill pe match karo; Verify HUID se check. [F4, F5]
takeaway: Sone ke bill mein sona plus banai ki fees, aur dono pe 3% tax.
follow_up: Papa — "HUID sahi hai, kaise pata?" → Verify HUID (BIS Care). [F5]

## Pick: #1 (wrong syllabus)
Why: everyone checks "aaj ka rate" and thinks that's the bill; the game maps 1:1 — rate = one chapter, making = out-of-syllabus question, GST 3% marks the whole paper incl. making, HUID = each piece's roll number that must match the bill. Retold to a parent it's still correct, and it has three true escalations.
