# Premise — October ki salary kam kyun aayi?

## Spine (no jokes)
1. PF is 12% of your basic wages (+DA), cut from your salary every month.
2. For years, PF was typically calculated only up to a ₹15,000 wage ceiling.
3. From 17 Sep 2026 the ceiling is ₹25,000; the 12% rate didn't change.
4. So if your basic is between ₹15k and ₹25k, more of it now goes to PF — October is the first full month.
5. In-hand drops, but that money is in your own PF account, not gone.

## 8 candidate games
1. **The ceiling is a literal chhat; PF stood stooped under it since 2014, the roof got raised, PF stretched (angdaai) — into your salary.** Heightens: stooped (₹1,800) → roof raised → stretches (₹2,640) → payoff: the stretch is inside your own ghar.
2. HR's "as per policy" translated three times, each translation one step deeper. Heightens by translation, but a game of only definitions.
3. PF as a roommate who used to pay rent on a small room, now moved into the bigger one. Muddy: roommate takes, doesn't save.
4. PF as a piggy bank whose coin slot got wider. Clear but static; no third step.
5. Salary SMS and offer letter as two different people who never talk. Observed, but explains CTC, not the ceiling.
6. PF as a tailor taking measurements only up to a tape length; new longer tape. Close to #1, less visual.
7. PF as an older sibling "rakh leta hoon" — too close to the taken mummy-lifafa game.
8. Salary as a thali, PF's katori got bigger. Food prop, generic.

## Pick: #1, with HR's "as per policy" as the plant/callback
The word "ceiling" literally becomes the explanation (chhat → limit → raised → PF grows into it), it has three
true steps backed by F1/F7, and the payoff flips it: the chhat went up but the money is still in your ghar (PF account).
HR's "as per policy" is planted in the hook and translated in the payoff.
