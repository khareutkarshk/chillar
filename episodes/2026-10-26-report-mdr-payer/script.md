## Hook
UPI ka naya charge — bill kiske naam pe hai?
Group chat: "UPI charge!! Aage bhejo!!" [JOKE] {meme: M5} {chillar: side-eye}

## Setup
NPCI ne bola yeh charge Diwali ke baad, date abhi nahi aayi. [F1] {meme: M7}
India ki national policy, ab official: "Diwali ke baad dekhte hain." [JOKE]
MDR ek fee hai: ₹2,000 se upar ki dukaan wali payment pe 0.4%. [F3]
Bill pe address likha hai: merchant. [F4] {stamp: "Merchant"}

## Escalation
Group ke bhai ka voice note: "₹2,000 se upar cash dena, fee nahi bharunga." [F3]
Bhai, tujhse maanga kisne? [JOKE] {chillar: facepalm}
Maine bhi ₹3,000 ke sneakers cart mein chhod diye. Fee bachane. Dukaan wale ki. [JOKE] [F8]
Hisaab simple — ₹3,000 ki sale, dukaan ke haath ₹2,988. [F8] {visual: receipt prints ₹3,000 − ₹12 = ₹2,988}
Aur main? Wahi ₹3,000, jo tag pe likha hai. [F8] [F4]
Dukaan ka aura minus ₹12. Mera? Wahi purana zero. [JOKE] {meme: M6} [F8]

## Payoff
Lagbhag 96% merchant payments ke liye yeh letter bana hi nahi. [F6]
Baaki pe address dukaan ka. Tumhara pincode nahi. [JOKE] [F4]
Rule: MDR dukaan ki kamai se katta hai, tumhare bill mein nahi judta. [F4] [F8] {stamp: "Bill pe nahi"}
Tumhare naam pe kya aaya? Sirf forward. [JOKE] {chillar: smug}
