## Hook
Mera ₹100 ka credit card tap — na OTP, na PIN, na guilt. [F11] [JOKE] {chillar: side-eye}
Par is ₹100 pe byaaj kab se lagta hai? [F11]

## Setup
Tap UPI jaisa lagta hai, par hai kirane wale uncle ka khata. [F2]
Paisa bank ka, naam mera. Udhaar. [F2]
Due date, yaani bill ki last date tak, poora chuka do, byaaj zero. [F2] {stamp: "BYAAJ ₹0"}
Maine "zero" suna, Diwali pe card aise nikala jaise uncle mere mama hon. [JOKE] {meme: M2}

## Escalation
Maan lo chai ₹100, auto ₹100 — itna toh chillar hai. [F11] [JOKE]
Mithai, diye, kurta, hamper, dinner, aur raat do baje "bas ek order". [F11] [JOKE] {visual: receipt prints all eight F11 lines, line by line}
Uncle ne chashma utaar ke bola, "Beta, ₹10,000." [F11] [JOKE] {meme: M4}
Uncle bole, "Minimum due de do, late nahi ginenge." [F4]
Par khata band nahi hoga. [F4] [JOKE]
Mummy ne poocha, "bill bhar diya?" Thumbs up bheja. Thumb ne jhooth bola. [JOKE]

## Payoff {field: note500}
Due date nikli, byaaj uss chai wale din se lag sakta hai. [F3] [JOKE] {chillar: crying}
RBI example mein ₹10,000 pe 2% byaaj, tax milake lagbhag ₹250. Ek mahine ka. [F12] {stamp: "~₹250"}
Poora bill, due date tak. Tabhi ₹100, ₹100 hai. [F2]
Rate? Har bank ka apna, RBI fix nahi karta. [F8]
Tap pe guilt nahi aata. Due date pe aata hai. [JOKE] {chillar: facepalm}
