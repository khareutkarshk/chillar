## Expert take
- myth: "Card swipe ₹100 hi toh hai, UPI jaisa — abhi kaun sa paisa gaya?"
- so what: A card swipe is a loan with a free window, not your own money going out. Pay the *entire* bill by the due date and it's interest-free [F2]; don't, and the free period is lost and interest may be charged from the transaction date [F3, F5]. Minimum due only keeps the bill from being "overdue" — it doesn't clear it [F4], and RBI makes every statement warn that minimum-only means compounding interest over months/years [F6].
- in ₹: Maan lo ₹10,000 of Diwali swipes [F11]: pay in full = ₹0 extra; leave it and RBI's own example shows about ₹250 for one month [F7, F12].
- do: This week open the card app, find the due date and "total amount due" (not "minimum due"), set a reminder two days before, and add up last month's swipes.
- follow-up: "Kitna interest lagta hai?" — RBI fixes no number; each issuer sets its own rate under its Board policy and must quote APR with examples [F8]. Read yours.
- jargon: due date → "bill bharne ki last date"; total amount due → "poora bill"; minimum amount due → "itna do toh late nahi gina jaayega, bill khatam nahi hota" [F4]; interest-free period → "free ka window"; APR → "saal bhar ka rate".
