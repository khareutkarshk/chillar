# Premise — Dhanteras emergency fund (30 s)

Spine: Dhanteras pe "dhan ghar lao" ka matlab sona/bartan samjha jaata hai. Layoff ke din cash is hafte chahiye.
PF slow hai: job-gap mein 75% turant, baaki 25% saal baad [F2]. Toh asli safety net alag cash fund hai.
Maan lo zaroori kharche ₹25,000 → ₹1,50,000 [F1]; ₹10,000 mahina → 15 mahine [F6]. Salary-day auto-transfer.

Candidates (game · how it heightens):
1. **Every Dhanteras "dhan" gets one test: "layoff ke baad kiraya bharega?"** · steel dabba fails → papa's PF half-fails (75% now, 25% next year) → the fund passes. ← PICKED
2. Fund = the notice period you give yourself · company gives "Quick sync", fund gives 6 months; heightens by month count.
3. Mummy's masala-dabba stash was the original emergency fund · stock-ish, weak escalation.
4. Layoff as a Diwali guest who arrives without calling · doesn't map to PF.
5. The landlord as the only person who doesn't accept shubh muhurat · explains rent, not the fund.
6. PF as a relative who promises help "agle saal" · cast-only, no fund mechanism.
7. Fund as a stepney tyre · clean, but a second image on top of Dhanteras.
8. Dhanteras group-chat poll where future-you votes down every option · too many words for 30 s.

Why 1: the test question IS the explanation (cash you can reach when the job goes); each step carries a fact
(PF 75/25 → ₹1,50,000 → 15 months); the payoff calls back the dabba. True when retold:
"Dhanteras ka asli dhan woh hai jo layoff ke baad kiraya bhare — PF poora turant nahi aata."
