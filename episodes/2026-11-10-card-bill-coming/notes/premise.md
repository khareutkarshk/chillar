# Premise — card bill coming (interest-free period)

Spine: (1) Myth: card shopping is free credit till the bill comes [F2]. (2) It's free only from swipe to due
date, and only if the *entire* bill is paid by then [F2]. (3) Miss that and the free period is gone; interest
may run from the swipe date, not the due date [F3]; an old balance also suspends next month's free period [F4].
(4) Maan lo ₹30,000 cart → ~₹750 first month vs ₹0 [F1]. (5) Pay total due, not min due [F7].

Candidates (what is the free period secretly behaving like?):
1. **A strict school library book** — return *all* books by the due date, fine zero; late, and ma'am counts the fine from the issue date; an old book pending = no new book free. Heightens: due date → fine from issue date → pending book blocks the next one.
2. Mall parking "first 2 hours free" — overstay a minute, charged from entry. Heightens to overstay; no clean map for "pay part" or the next-month suspension.
3. Hotel "free cancellation till 6 pm" — miss it, the whole stay is charged from check-in. Same gap on partial pay.
4. OTT free trial — forget to cancel, charged. Mapping is backwards (here you must act to stay free, inverted logic retells wrong).
5. Mummy's "fridge ki mithai guests ke liye hai" — free till guests arrive. Cute, no mechanism.
6. Gully-cricket "trial ball" — taken (digital-gold reel).
7. Kirana khata — taken (card-swipes-bill reel).
8. A wedding "free" buffet that bills you per plate if you leave before the photo — absurd, mechanism wrong.

Pick: **#1 library**. Every rule of the free period has a 1:1 library twin (due date = due date, "saari kitaabein" = entire
outstanding, fine from issue date = interest from swipe date, pending book = suspended free period), so a teen who retells only
the metaphor still says the right thing. Runner: my three identical rangoli stencils in the cart (plant → tag → callback).
Library and the librarian ma'am are new to the joke log; Diwali is overused, so it's never named — only the stencils hint at it.
