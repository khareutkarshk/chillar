## Expert take
- myth: "Card se Diwali ki shopping free credit hai — bill aane tak paisa mera." [F2]
- so what: The free period is conditional, not a feature. It exists only if you clear the *entire* bill by the due date; miss that and you lose it on the whole amount, and interest may run from the day you swiped, not from the due date. [F3, F4]
- in ₹: One ₹30,000 Diwali cart unpaid costs about ₹750 in the first month (₹600 + ~₹150 charges), against ₹0 if paid in full. That's ₹750 for doing nothing; if it kept running at that rate, ₹7,200 a year. [F1]
- do: This week, open the card app, note the due date, and set a reminder for "pay total due", not "min due", two days before; keep the money in your account.
- follow-up: Papa: "Minimum pay kar diya toh theek hai na?" — No: minimum due only stops the bill being treated as overdue; it isn't clearing it, and the interest-free period is still lost. [F7, F4]
- jargon: interest-free period → "bill aane se due date tak ka free wait"; total due → "poora bill, ek paisa kam nahi"; minimum due → "bas late-fine se bachne wala hissa"; outstanding → "jo abhi bhi baaki hai"; APR → "saal bhar ka interest rate".
