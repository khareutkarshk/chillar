# Premise — 2026-10-credit-enquiry

## Spine (no jokes)
1. Your score is one 300–900 number; your report is the file behind it.
2. Checking your own score is a look (soft) — RBI's rules even name a "soft enquiry – score unaffected" category.
3. Applying for a card/loan = a lender pulls your report; that enquiry is the one that counts, and the bureau alerts you.
4. Four applications in one festive week = four enquiries in a few days, and each new lender sees the others.
5. Rejections aren't reported, but the enquiries are; your own full report is free once a year from each bureau.

## 8 candidate games
1. **Report = your social profile.** Opening your own profile = nobody knows (soft); applying = a follow request / "hey" DM that pings and is remembered (hard); 4 cards = "hey" to 4 people in a week — and each one can see the other three. Heightens: own view → one request → four, all visible to each other.
2. Society watchman's register: entering your own flat isn't logged, visitors are; 4 visitors in a week and the watchman calls a society meeting. Clear, less Gen Z.
3. Weighing scale: standing on it doesn't make you fat. Kills the myth but has no step 2/3.
4. Rishta background check: families "dekhne aate hain"; 4 families in a week = desperation. Dated prop, gender-risky.
5. Job hunt: applying to 4 companies, each HR sees the others. Clear but flat.
6. Exam result: checking marks vs re-evaluation. Doesn't map hard enquiry.
7. Aura points: every application spends aura. Slang, not an explanation.
8. Nani ke nuskhe: score superstition as home remedies. Good for one act-out, not a whole reel.

## Pick: #1
It maps every fact one-to-one (own view = soft, follow request = hard, notification = bureau SMS [F3],
"left on read isn't screenshotted" = rejections not reported [F5], everyone sees the list = clustering [F9]),
and step 3 (every crush sees who else you DMed) is absurd and literally true for lenders. #8 is used as
the one act-out (mausi's "nazar lagti hai") and becomes the payoff callback.
