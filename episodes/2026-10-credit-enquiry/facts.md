## Texture for the writers (not facts — don't put numbers from here on screen)
- Festive season = every shopping site pushes "apply card, get ₹X voucher / extra discount"; the checkout button feels like free money.
- Gen Z first-card people check their score obsessively on a finance app, like checking a crush's last-seen — then panic that checking itself "cost" points.
- Credit score discourse is half superstition: "mat check karna", "ek card kam karo, score badhega" passed down like nani ke nuskhe.
- The 4-cards-in-4-days person thinks each approval is a win; each rejection is a quiet text message they don't forward to the group chat.
- Relatives ask "CIBIL kitna hai?" at the Diwali table right after "package kitna hai?".
- Rejection SMS arrives in the middle of a cart; they keep shopping anyway.
