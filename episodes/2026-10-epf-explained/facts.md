## Worked example (F19) — computed with python, rounded to whole rupees

Assumptions, state plainly if shown:
- Basic ₹46,250/month (12 LPA CTC at 50% basic). Employee 12% = ₹5,550 + employer 12% = ₹5,550 → ₹11,100/month into EPF (new joiner above the ceiling, not an EPS member, so no pension share).
- Interest rate 8.25% a year, held flat for all years (F1; the rate is declared yearly and can change).
- Each month's contribution is added at month end; interest for the month = that month's closing balance × 8.25% / 12 (monthly running balance, F6); the year's interest is added to the balance once, at year end, and earns interest from the next year (credited annually).
- Flat case: no raises. Raise case: contribution rises 5% every year (₹11,100 in year 1, ₹11,655 in year 2, ...).
- No withdrawals, no tax, no employer cap on wages. If instead interest started from the month after contribution, results are about 0.5% lower (30 yr flat: ₹1,63,96,409).

```
Years   Balance (flat ₹11,100/month)   Total put in      Interest earned
1       ₹1,39,152                      ₹1,33,200         ₹5,952
5       ₹8,20,431                      ₹6,66,000         ₹1,54,431
10      ₹20,39,930                     ₹13,32,000        ₹7,07,930
30      ₹1,65,05,027                   ₹39,96,000        ₹1,25,09,027

30 years with 5% yearly raises: ₹2,76,74,110   (put in: ₹88,49,654; interest: ₹1,88,24,456)
```

Withdrawal cost (same model, for the payoff's phone strip): a lump sum earns its full 8.25% for every
complete year it stays in (monthly running balance × 8.25%/12 × 12, credited at year end), so ₹X left in for
n years grows to X × 1.0825^n. A ₹1,00,000 withdrawal (example phone price, our assumption) is
₹1,00,000 × 1.0825^30 = ₹10,78,542 less in the account 30 years later (₹2,20,942 after 10 years,
₹4,88,155 after 20). Python: `round(100000 * 1.0825**30)` → 1078542. Holds only if the rate stays 8.25%.
Rounded for screen: ~₹10.8 lakh.

Rounded for screen: ~₹1.4 lakh · ~₹8.2 lakh · ~₹20.4 lakh · ~₹1.65 crore · ~₹2.77 crore (raises).
Interest is about 3 of every 4 rupees at 30 years in the flat case (₹1.25 crore of ₹1.65 crore).
