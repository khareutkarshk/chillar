## Premise verdict (the creator's question)
The whodunit frame is **a costume, not a premise.** A crime board with red string, a detective hat and "saboot ek"
is the stock "explainer dressed as a detective" reel; it's already been done and it doesn't change the
explanation at all. Take the hat off and you're left with the usual CTC-vs-in-hand list. The frame also doesn't
pay off its own structure: there's one suspect (Income Tax), no lineup, "saboot ek" with no "saboot do", no
"case closed", and the culprit reveal ("offer letter") is a thing, not a character. Nobody gasps at "it was the
document".
The **actually original, Dayama-tier idea is already in the script and it's buried: Mummy has distributed mithai
for ₹1 lakh/month.** It's specific, it's desi, it's what makes someone DM the reel to a sibling, and it carries
the CTA ("mummy ko bhejo"). Right now it appears in two lines and a sticker. Pick one:
(a) commit to the whodunit for real (suspects lineup incl. Income Tax + something unexpected, Tax walks on an
alibi, the culprit is a character you can stamp, e.g. CTC unmasked as "Cost to Company"), or
(b) drop the detective and make the mithai the engine (each deduction costs mithai from the dabba in front of
the relatives). (b) is cheaper in words and more ours. Writer's call, but the current half-and-half doesn't work.
