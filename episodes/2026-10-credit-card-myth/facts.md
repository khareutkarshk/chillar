## Texture for the writers (not facts — don't put numbers from here on screen)
- First card = parent panic: "card liya? ab toh karza pakka" — they treat the plastic itself as the loan, not the unpaid balance.
- The bill SMS lands with two numbers, "Total due" and "Minimum due"; the small one looks friendlier, so it gets clicked. Everyone's first month: "minimum bhar diya, sorted".
- "Abhi card, baad mein EMI" energy; credit limit reads like free pocket money until the statement date.
- Autopay set to minimum feels responsible — it's the trap wearing a seatbelt. Autopay on full is the boring hero.
- Callback: reel 1's 12 LPA offer letter — salary arrives, card bill is the first thing that tries to leave with it.
- Friends split dinners by putting everything on one card and collecting UPI later; fine if the full bill is cleared.
