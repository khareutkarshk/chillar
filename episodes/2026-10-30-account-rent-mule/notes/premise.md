# Premise — account rent / mule accounts (30 s)

## Spine (no jokes)
1. A "part-time job" offer asks you to let strangers move money through your savings account for a commission (example, F4).
2. Their money comes in and goes out; they stay anonymous. The account, the name and the KYC are yours.
3. Banks now run AI fraud-detection to catch mule accounts (F1).
4. More than 26.48 lakh mule accounts have already been shared via I4C's Suspect Registry (F2).
5. So: never rent out account/card/SIM/OTP, report the recruiter on 1930 (F3), warn the group.

## 8 candidate games
1. **Group project:** they do the "work", only your name is on the cover page → you were always that guy in college → but this project gets graded by bank AI → the class list is called "Suspect Registry" → callback: this reel is the one group project where everyone's name goes on.
2. Hostel visitor register: whoever signs, the warden calls → heightens to strangers signing in your name daily → weaker for money flow.
3. Account as a rented flat: tenant runs something shady, police knock on the owner → landlord frame is too old-person.
4. You as the "brand ambassador" with zero salary slip → ambassador jokes are dated.
5. Relay race where you're the only runner with a jersey number → visual, but hard to say spoken.
6. Lending your ID card to a proxy-attendance friend → close to #1 but smaller stakes, doesn't escalate.
7. Getaway driver who doesn't know there's a heist → film parody, edges into how-it-works detail.
8. CC'd on an email thread you never read → too office-y for a hostel group.

**Pick: #1.** Every student has lived it (observed truth), it explains the mechanism exactly (they act, your name carries it), and it escalates on facts: cover page → AI grader (F1) → the list is literally named "Suspect Registry" (F2) → reversal callback "kaam tumhara, naam sabka".

- "Hostel group ka sabse aasaan part-time job ek group project hai." (laugh, but no stakes)
