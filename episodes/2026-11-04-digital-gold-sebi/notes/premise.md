# Premise — digital gold & SEBI (30 s, Myth vs Math)

Spine: Myth: "app pe gold hai, SEBI-regulated hoga." · SEBI (Nov 2025) said digital gold is neither a security
nor a commodity derivative, so it's outside its purview [F1][F2]. · So SEBI's investor-protection mechanisms
don't apply [F4]; the risk is the seller/vault company not delivering [F3]. · ₹10 or more, the protection
question is the same [F4][F7]. · SEBI's own gold routes exist: Gold ETFs, EGRs, exchange derivatives via
registered intermediaries [F5].

Candidates (premise → how it heightens):
1. **Digital gold is gully cricket: no umpire, and the ball belongs to the seller** → SEBI-umpire says "not my
   match" → no appeal (protection) → ball-owner goes home (counterparty) → stadium matches exist (F5).
2. The "24K" badge is aura farming → looks official → SEBI says not ours → aura minus. (badge only; thin mechanism)
3. SEBI is the society guard; digital gold is the stall outside the gate → guard can't help → stall shuts. (true, flat)
4. Digital gold is tuition outside school; the principal can't punish the tuition sir. (mechanism fuzzy)
5. The app is a friend "keeping your gold safe, trust me" → screenshots as proof → number changed. (counterparty only)
6. SEBI is the exam board; digital gold an unaffiliated certificate. (exam shape taken by gold-bill reel)
7. A WhatsApp-group kitty with the admin as banker → admin leaves. (sounds like "scam" — SEBI never said that)
8. A hostel locker with no warden → warden says "not my hostel". (close to #3)

Pick: #1. Every step is true when retold ("SEBI digital gold ka umpire nahi; uski protection nahi; risk seller ka;
SEBI wale stadium alag hain"), "jiski ball uska match" is something every Indian kid has lived, and
"illegal nahi bola, bola mera match nahi" answers the dad's first question.
