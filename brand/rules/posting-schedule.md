# Posting schedule — one Reel every day

One timetable for the week: what goes out each day, when it's made, and when it's posted.
Post time: **7:30 PM IST** every day (Sunday 11:00 AM). After 3 weeks, check Instagram Insights →
"Most active times" for @chillar.ind and move the slot to where the followers actually are.

Per week: 3 × 30 s · 3 × 60 s · 1 × 90 s. The theme column is a default — the director still picks
by the script's mood (CLAUDE.md §1). The 90 s sits on Sunday morning, when people have time to watch
a long reel; Saturday evening is the weakest slot, so it gets a 30 s.
**Topic beats weekday:** if the week's most urgent topic doesn't fit that day's series, run it
anyway under the series it fits.

### Topic test: does this topic feel real or like AI slop?
**Freshness first:** if most finance pages have already covered it (news more than ~5 days old, or a
scam everyone has warned about since last year), it gets one line in the Monday Report, not its own
reel. The exception is an angle nobody has done yet. Look for news that changes the viewer's own salary slip, bill or account
*this month*, before they notice it themselves.

A topic goes in only if it passes **4 of 5**:
1. **A real trigger:** it happened this month, a date forces it (salary day, Diwali, a deadline), or
   it's a question people really ask (comments, DMs, family WhatsApp).
2. **It costs or saves the viewer real money, or protects them**, and they can check or do something today.
3. **One surprising number from a primary source** (RBI, NPCI, PIB, I4C, CBDT), not a blog.
4. **A specific person to send it to** (fill the send-to column). If nobody comes to mind, it won't get shared.
5. **A scene only an Indian 22-year-old would recognise:** the hostel group, the office Diwali gift
   hamper, the uncle who forwards everything.

**Auto-reject (generic, sounds AI-made):** "5 money tips", "power of compounding", "rich vs poor
mindset", "50/30/20 budgeting", "passive income", "financial freedom", "stop buying coffee",
anything that could be posted unchanged in any country or any month.

### Growth habits for the sprint (on top of the daily reel)
- **Write every script for sends:** one "this is my friend" line per reel. Sends and saves count for
  more than likes when Instagram decides whether to show a reel to non-followers.
- **Hashtags: 3–5 specific ones only** (Instagram caps posts at 5 since Dec 2025). Put the topic's
  plain keywords in the caption's first line (salary, gold, credit card, Diwali bonus), because search reads them.
- **Facebook gets its own caption** in Meta Business Suite: shorter, Hindi-leaning, no hashtags.
  Check it posts as a native Reel on the Page.
- **Daily Story:** a poll or quiz on the reel's number 2–3 h before posting ("12 LPA pe in-hand kitna?"),
  then share the reel to Stories once it's live.
- **Sunday carousel** (25 Oct, 1 Nov, 15 Nov): 5–7 slides of the week's receipts and passbook frames
  from the contact sheets + one maths slide. Made to be saved, at almost no production cost.
- **Trial Reels** (if available on the account): run experiments here (a new hook style or a
  different length) instead of in the main slot.
- **Post-time test:** 24 Oct – 1 Nov at 7:30 PM, 2 – 15 Nov (non-festival days) at 9 PM. Compare
  non-follower reach for the two slots.
- **Comments:** reply to every one in the first hour; turn the best question into a future topic.

## Weekly rhythm
- **Sunday, 30 min:** skim the week's RBI/NPCI/PIB releases and your comments, then pick the 7 topics
  for next week. Each must pass the topic test above (one concept per reel, no repeats within 4 weeks); run `pipeline/facts_library.py stale` and re-verify anything stale
  before the week starts.
- **Every day, 10 min after posting:** reply to comments for the first hour; note topic requests in
  the backlog.
- **Saturday:** `npx ccusage@20.0.28 daily` for the week — cost per reel should trend down.
- **Buffer:** keep 2 finished reels in `episodes/` that aren't date-bound (concepts, myths). If a day's
  reel fails review after 3 rounds, post a buffer reel and finish the other one tomorrow.
- **Around 1 Feb (Union Budget):** the Monday Report covers it; every `budget` entry in the facts
  library is stale until re-verified — no tax reel ships before that.
